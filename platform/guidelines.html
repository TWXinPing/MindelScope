Ten rules (v0.1)

  1. Type is carried by shape, not only by colour.
  2. Every module has a computational goal, an equation or explicit “none”, and a situation mapping.
  3. Gating, information, error, and context are different edge types.
  4. Hypothesis distributions are first-class nodes.
  5. Time is a player, not a caption: bind traces or say that the figure is static.
  6. Paper architecture and this implementation may differ; unimplemented parts are dashed and labelled.
  7. The plant / world is drawn if the model claims to act on one.
  8. A scenario script must describe the same world the simulator actually runs.
  9. Classic models default to unofficial until the original team confirms meaning.
  10. Export a static SVG for the paper and keep the interactive page as supplementary.
