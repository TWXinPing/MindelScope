MindelSpec v0 glyph set

Use these shapes the same way in every model. Colour is secondary: module identity can use colour, but type must still be readable in greyscale.

process — a transformation (forward model, inverse model, predictor).
representation — a state, goal, or contextual cue.
comparator — a prediction or control error (circular summing junction).
gate — a responsibility-weighted mix or selection.
distribution — a hypothesis distribution (λ, softmax, prior).
delay — hold a signal so two times can meet (predicted x̂_t+1 vs actual x_t+1), or sensory / feedback lag.
plant — body or world, not an internal model.

Edges

information — solid arrow. gating — width tracks λ. error — rust. context — dashed, often paper-only in this demo.

Grey dashed nodes are in the source paper but not in this implementation. Toggle “Paper architecture” in the viewer to restore them.
